# Design principles

Hyprcraft should feel coherent and intentional rather than like a pile of unrelated widgets.

## Principles

1. **Hierarchy before decoration** — important information is immediately legible.
2. **Restraint** — use color, blur, borders and motion with purpose.
3. **Consistency** — shared patterns behave and look alike across surfaces.
4. **Density with breathing room** — compact does not mean cramped.
5. **Keyboard parity** — visible controls should have predictable keyboard behavior.
6. **Responsive by default** — layouts should adapt to different resolutions and scale factors.
7. **Performance is part of design** — avoid needless polling, animations and heavyweight effects.

## Design workflow

Observe a real problem, describe the user task, sketch the hierarchy, define the interaction, then implement. Do not begin by choosing a palette.

## Decision record

The initial visual direction is intentionally undecided. Record meaningful decisions in this folder when they are made, including the trade-offs and the components affected.
